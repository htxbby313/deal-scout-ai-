About Coleman & Co.

One parent company. Different businesses. One standard.

Coleman & Co. Holdings LLC serves as the parent company for a portfolio of independently positioned ventures. Each business has its own customer, purpose, identity, and growth strategy.

The holding company provides shared structure, planning, technology, research, marketing, financial discipline, and operational support without requiring every brand to look or operate the same way.

The result is a portfolio designed to let individual businesses serve their markets well while benefiting from the resources and stability of the larger company.
