# Deal Scout methodology alignment

## Controlling operating sequence

1. Find and research a seller opportunity using attributable public evidence.
2. Establish a respectful seller relationship and understand authority, motivation, timing, pain points, and minimum acceptable outcome.
3. Analyze the property and economics without presenting projections as guaranteed profit.
4. Obtain the necessary individualized, jurisdiction-approved, executed contractual interest.
5. Match the property internally to verified developer and investor buy boxes.
6. Present the property only after contract, disclosure, counsel, compliance, owner, and disposition controls permit it.
7. Coordinate title/closing through approved professionals and providers.
8. Record revenue only from settlement-backed evidence.
9. Preserve the relationship and repeat the process.

This maps the owner-approved public operating sequence—find seller, contract, buyer, title, fee, repeat—into Deal Scout’s evidence and transaction controls.

Educational examples never become verified financial inputs, legal conclusions, required disclosures, or jurisdiction rules. Supplied course materials and contract forms must be reviewed and mapped separately before they can affect activated transaction behavior.
